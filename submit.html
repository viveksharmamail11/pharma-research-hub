Before submitting

  1. Read the author guidelines and journal scope.
  2. Remove author-identifying information from the blinded manuscript if required.
  3. Prepare a cover letter and author contribution details.
  4. Confirm ethics approval or exemption where applicable.
  5. Disclose funding and conflicts of interest.
  6. Ensure all authors approve the submitted version.
