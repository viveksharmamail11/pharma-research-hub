Submission checklist

Prepare before you send

Manuscript

Title, abstract, keywords, main text, tables/figures and references in the requested format.

Author details

Names, affiliations, corresponding-author contact and contribution information.

Declarations

Conflicts of interest, funding, ethics approval, consent and other required statements.

File quality

Use readable files and ensure figures, references and supplementary material are complete.

Recommended workflow

  1. Read the journal scope and policies.
  2. Prepare the manuscript and declarations.
  3. Complete the submission form.
  4. Editorial screening.
  5. Peer review where applicable.
  6. Revision and final decision.
  7. Production and publication.
