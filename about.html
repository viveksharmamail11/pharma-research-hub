About the journal

Knowledge with purpose.
Evidence with integrity.

Journal of Pharmaceutical Research & Innovation (JP+) is a proposed independent scholarly publication focused on the dissemination of sound research in pharmaceutical and allied health sciences.

Aims & scope

Connecting research and practice

The journal aims to provide a forum for original research, review articles, case reports, and brief communications that contribute to pharmaceutical knowledge, healthcare practice, and scientific methods.

Areas of interest include pharmaceutics and drug delivery, pharmacology and toxicology, pharmaceutical analysis and chemistry, pharmacognosy, clinical and hospital pharmacy, pharmacovigilance, regulatory science, biotechnology, and pharmacy education.

Publication model

  • Open-access intent
  • Editorial screening and peer review
  • Online-first publication model
  • Publication schedule to be announced

Do not advertise a review timeline, indexing status, ISSN, DOI, or fee waiver until confirmed.

Editorial standards

Built on responsible publishing

Research integrity

Authors must submit original work, accurately report methods and results, and disclose relevant conflicts of interest.

Fair assessment

Editorial decisions should be based on scholarly merit, relevance, methodological quality, and journal scope.

Clear accountability

Policies should explain authorship, corrections, complaints, appeals, and handling of suspected misconduct.
