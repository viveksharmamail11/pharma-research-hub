Editorial policies

Trust is part of
the research.

These policy summaries are a starting framework. The editorial team must approve and operationalize them before accepting submissions.

01

Peer review

Submissions undergo an initial editorial assessment for scope, completeness, ethical declarations, and basic scholarly suitability. Manuscripts proceeding beyond screening are evaluated by qualified reviewers. Editors make the final decision and should manage conflicts of interest.

02

Publication ethics

Authors are responsible for originality, accurate reporting, appropriate permissions, ethical approvals, and disclosure of funding and competing interests. Suspected plagiarism, fabrication, falsification, duplicate submission, or authorship disputes should be assessed through a documented procedure.

03

Corrections and retractions

The journal should maintain a transparent process for correcting significant errors and retracting articles when warranted. Notices should identify the affected article and explain the reason without obscuring the scholarly record.

04

Authorship and contributions

All listed authors should meet the journal's stated authorship criteria, approve the final manuscript, and accept accountability for their contributions. Changes to authorship after submission require written agreement from all affected authors.

05

Open access and fees

Clearly state the license, reuse permissions, copyright arrangements, and any submission or publication charges. If no fees are charged, confirm this policy and disclose any future changes before they take effect.

06

Complaints and appeals

Provide a named editorial contact and a fair process for authors to raise concerns about editorial decisions, review conduct, or publication ethics. Maintain records of outcomes.
